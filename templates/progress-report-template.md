## Reporting rules

- Never invent precision. Use a range or maturity label when evidence is mixed.
- Do not count planned work as implemented.
- Do not count code as verified without acceptance evidence.
- Put caveats beside the numbers they qualify.
- Attribute historical metrics to their source and date.
- Include every canonical workstream or explain its exclusion.
- End with one next step, not a wish list.
