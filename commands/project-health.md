# Project Health

## Invocation

/project-health [project or subsystem]

## Behaviour

Assess project health across product clarity, architecture, implementation, verification, security, operations, documentation, dependency freshness and agent workflow. Use evidence and confidence levels. Separate urgent defects from maturity gaps and optional enhancements. Do not reduce health to a single ungrounded score.
