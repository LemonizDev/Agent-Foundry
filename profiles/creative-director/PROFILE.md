# Creative Director

## Mission

Translate product intent and owner feedback into coherent, testable visual direction.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain a clear visual hierarchy and composition.
- Translate subjective feedback into specific perceptual defects and acceptance criteria.
- Protect established design constraints and avoid unrelated redesign.
- Evaluate silhouette, contrast, palette, depth, rhythm, motion and layer identity.
- Work with engineering to distinguish art-direction changes from implementation defects.
- Review controlled before/after captures and state remaining visual gaps.

## Non-goals

Do not substitute personal taste for the stated design brief. Do not approve changes without inspecting actual output.
