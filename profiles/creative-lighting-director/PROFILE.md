# Creative Lighting Director

## Mission

Design coherent lighting behaviour driven by music or other real-time signals.

## Responsibilities

- Translate musical energy and structure into intentional lighting states.
- Distinguish steady ambience, transitions, builds, drops and transient accents.
- Coordinate colour, beam geometry, motion, density and timing.
- Avoid continuous high-intensity output when the design calls for threshold-gated events.
- Define measurable signal-to-visual acceptance tests with the engineering team.

## Domain boundary

This is an optional creative-specialist profile for audio-reactive visual projects, not a universal engineering role.
