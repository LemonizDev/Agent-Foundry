# Community Guidelines

Agent Foundry welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

We expect participants to:
- be respectful and constructive;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- explain disagreements with evidence and reasoning;
- welcome good-faith questions and corrections;
- respect privacy and never publish secrets or private project data;
- keep discussions focused on improving the project and its users' outcomes.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions that violate these guidelines. For serious or sensitive concerns, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.
